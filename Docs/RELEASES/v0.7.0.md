# ASHEN VAULTS v0.7.0 — «Враждебные глубины»

**Status:** implementation in C++ source, NOT a validated UE4.27 build. Dedicated enemy sprites, physical camp doors, advanced squad coordination and full QA remain pending.

## Enemy levels and difficulty
- Enemy level = 1 + (floor - 1) * 2 + random variation 0–2.
- Spawn groups grow with depth: 2–8 enemies per ordinary chamber depending on floor and RNG.
- Max HP gains a multiplier of 1 + 0.13 * (level - 1); damage gains 1 + 0.085 * (level - 1).
- Existing depth-based health multiplier and elite modifiers still apply. Bosses also receive levels.

## Enemy types
New ordinary archetypes: rat (15), zombie (16), skeleton (17), ghost (18), witch (19), crypt guard (20), druid (21), wretch (22), stone golem (23), iron golem (24). Existing cinder golem (7) remains distinct. Bandit archer (25) and raider (33) form the rival camp faction.

Seven miniboss templates (26–32): Grave Butcher, Bone Archer, Rot Witch, Stone Sentinel, Blood Reaver, Cinder Shaman, Iron Tyrant. Optional miniboss encounter chance is 55% per floor, with distinct baseline health, speed, damage, ranged/melee flag and projectile spread/cadence parameters. These are **not** seven fully authored unique ability sets.

## Equipment and loot
- Approximately 8.5% of normally spawned enemies carry equipment (armor, bow, enchanted weapon).
- Armor increases HP by 35%; enchanted weapons increase attack damage by 25%.
- A bow-equipped zombie currently changes to the bandit-archer archetype, so the dedicated zombie-bow appearance is NOT implemented.
- On death, equipped enemies have a 14% chance to drop an existing shard reward representing their gear. Full wearable equipment and inventory are NOT implemented.

## Rival bandit hideout
- On eligible maps, a 38% chance selects an existing chamber for a bandit camp with 3–6 raiders/archers and two non-mimic chests.
- Bandits and dungeon enemies can target and damage each other; both remain hostile to the player.
- This is a **room encounter**, not a newly generated enclosed room with functioning doors. Doors are still pending.

## Enemy AI
- Short-term last-seen-target memory (3.5 seconds), line-of-sight checks.
- Some agile archetypes strafe while close; allied enemies apply spacing to reduce stacking.
- Rival faction target selection at up to 7 tile distance.
- Miniboss projectile fan spreads and different cooldowns; boss phases from prior versions retained.

## Rendering
- HUD displays level numbers, equipped markers and color strips for new archetypes.
- New types currently reuse a placeholder sprite. Unique authored sprites/animations not yet included.
