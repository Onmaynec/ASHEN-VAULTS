# ASHEN VAULTS v0.7.1 — «Охотники глубин»

**C++ source update on main.** Unreal Engine 4.27 Windows compilation and in-game QA have not been performed in this environment; this is NOT a certified working build.

## Visuals and animation
- 23 enemy archetypes (IDs 11–33) now have their own procedurally drawn 16×16 pixel silhouettes with distinct species equipment, eyes, robes, skulls, golem seams and bandit details.
- Each receives a second walking frame, selected at runtime with per-enemy phase offset.
- Mini-bosses have crown/horn markers, elites have accents, and armored, bow-carrying and enchanted-weapon enemies display equipment overlays.
- Dedicated pixel-art pickup icons for chestplate, bow and enchanted weapon.
- Special attacks show windup bars and directional target indicators. Camp palisades, gates and three trap variants have separate visuals.

## Combat AI
- Vision detection limited to 11 tiles, with line-of-sight obstruction.
- Last-known-target memory (3.5s) and cached BFS navigation to that remembered location.
- Allies broadcast sightings to nearby teammates (5.5 tiles); some agile types flank, dodge and strafe; pack hunters converge; allied spacing reduces stacking.
- Bandits and dungeon enemies fight with physical projectile collision and melee attacks. Both target the player.
- Shielded crypt guards and armor-wearing enemies mitigate frontal melee damage.
- Armed zombies retain their zombie sprite and shoot when carrying a bow.
- Elite Ashlings gain a telegraphed triple slash; stone, iron and cinder golems gain distinct radial slams; witches and druids have elemental volleys; ghosts blink.

## Seven minibosses: separate abilities
- Grave Butcher: close-range five-blade cleave.
- Bone Archer: fast triple arrow volley.
- Rot Witch: poisonous five-shot fan.
- Stone Sentinel: twelve-direction radial shockwave.
- Blood Reaver: short dash with triple blade fan.
- Cinder Shaman: eight-way burning crossfire.
- Iron Tyrant: tight five-shot molten salvo.
- Each has its own HP, speed, damage, attack cadence and enrage cooldown below 45% HP.
- Attacks are telegraphed. Mini-bosses grant increased loot rewards.

## Existing bosses improved
- Bone Regent summons skeleton reinforcements.
- Forge Tyrant spreads persistent molten hazards.
- Vault Warden repositions with a phase blink.
- All three use periodic radial patterns and accelerated casts when low on health.

## Bandit camp and generation
- A randomly chosen room is enclosed by wooden palisades; every intersecting corridor becomes a closed gate.
- Press F near a gate to open it, update navigation and enter the hostile camp.
- Bandit raiders and archers guard two non-mimic loot chests.
- Added extra cross-room loops, interior stone pillars and three trap types: spikes, flame vents and dart launchers.

## Equipment drops
- Approximately 8.5% of naturally generated enemies carry armor, a bow or enchanted weapon.
- Equipment has gameplay effects: armor increases enemy max HP and can block frontal attacks; bows grant ranged attacks; enchanted weapons increase enemy damage.
- A defeated equipped enemy has a 14% chance to drop salvageable gear.
- Player chestplate: +8% damage reduction per pickup, capped at 45%; bow: +2 thrown-blade damage; weapon: +3 melee attack. All are run-only bonuses.
- HUD shows armor, ranged bonus and gear pickup count.

## Progression
- Enemy level increases by two per floor, with a random 0–2 level spread.
- Per-level HP +13%, damage +8.5%, in addition to existing depth and elite multipliers.
- Groups increase with depth, up to 8 enemies per ordinary room.

## QA status / known limitations
- Updated standalone content assertions but did not execute them; UE4.27 build not available.
- Sprites are procedural 16×16 Canvas textures with two-frame motion, **not** imported Paper2D Flipbooks or hand-painted animation sheets.
- Mini-boss spells are distinct, but combat balance and performance require actual playtesting.
- Bandit camps use tile gates and wall tiles, not fully independent authored level assets.
- No claim of tested executable or fully verified gameplay. Full Unreal compilation, gameplay QA, and tuning remain required before declaring this release production-ready.
